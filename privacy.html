Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Brick & Bun, create an account, save delivery addresses in Austin, or place an order, we collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and delivery instructions. We also collect transaction details and technical data through cookies and session storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to process and fulfill your food delivery orders, communicate order statuses, manage your user profile and saved delivery locations, send operational notifications, and improve our website and kitchen services.

3. Data Security and Sharing

Your data is securely stored using modern backend infrastructure (such as Supabase). We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only with essential service providers (such as payment processors like Stripe) strictly required to execute your transactions.

4. Your Choices and Rights

You can update or correct your profile details and saved delivery addresses at any time through your account dashboard. You may also request the permanent deletion of your account and associated data directly from your account settings.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Continued use of our platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@brickandbun.com.
