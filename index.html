About

Brick & Bun started with a brick oven. Ninety-gram smash burgers seared hot and smoked for four hours over oak. Local Austin beef, brioche toasted in butter, and takeout only.

No dining room. No waiting around. Just the essentials: smoke, brick, and brioche.
